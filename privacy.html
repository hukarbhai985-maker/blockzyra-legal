BLOCKZYRA – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  |  App: BLOCKZYRA (com.blockzyra.game)

This policy explains what information the BLOCKZYRA mobile game ("the game", "we") uses and why. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we use

  • Account information. If you sign up with email, we process your email address and password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address and a unique account ID. If you play as a guest, an anonymous ID is created for you.
  • Profile information. Your nickname and your profile picture (an emoji or an image you choose from your gallery).
  • Game data. Your coins, diamonds (gems), scores, number of games played, settings and progress.
  • Leaderboard data. Your nickname, best score and number of games played are shown publicly to other players on the leaderboard. Your email address is never shown.
  • Device storage. Some preferences and progress are stored on your device so the game works offline.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in again.
  • To save your progress, coins and diamonds so you can continue later.
  • To show the leaderboard, daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • To provide support when you contact us.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse.

3. Services we rely on

The game uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) to sign you in and store your data, and Google Sign-In if you choose it. These services are provided by Google and process data under their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising and payments. We may add advertising (Google AdMob) and in-game purchases (such as Google Play Billing or Xsolla) in the future. If we do, those providers may process device identifiers, advertising IDs and purchase information under their own policies, and we will update this page before they are enabled.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above (to run the game), when required by law, or to protect the game and its players from fraud or abuse. Leaderboard nickname and scores are visible to all players.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and game data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it at any time by emailing skom9854@gmail.com from the email address of your account (or send your nickname if you play as a guest). We will delete it within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. You can also clear the data stored on your device with "Reset Progress" in Settings, or by uninstalling the game.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that each player can only read and change their own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

BLOCKZYRA is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • Play as a guest without giving an email address.
  • Change your nickname and picture in the game.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by email.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: skom9854@gmail.com
